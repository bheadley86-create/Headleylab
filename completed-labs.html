What These Labs Reinforced

  • Troubleshooting is more effective when each layer is tested separately.
  • DNS, addressing, routing, ports, services, permissions, and applications can fail independently.
  • Verification is necessary after every configuration change.
  • Recovery planning and documentation are part of normal system administration.
  • Structured labs are most valuable when paired with independent hands-on projects and real troubleshooting.
